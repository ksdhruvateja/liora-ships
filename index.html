About Liora Ships

A Legacy of Moving What Matters

Founded in 2008, Liora Ships has grown from a regional forwarder into a global logistics powerhouse operating in more than 180 countries. We combine deep industry expertise with modern technology to deliver freight solutions that keep supply chains moving.
